Confidential · Product description

ReWorth

Lagos, your unused things are worth something.

Nigeria’s trusted recommerce marketplace — buy, sell, swap, or give away what you already own. Photograph it. List it in about a minute. Trade nearby with more confidence than WhatsApp.

For founders, partners, ops, and anyone who needs to understand the product · 2026

The problem

Useful things sit idle. Resale is messy.

Households hold furniture, phones, generators, fashion, cars, and office kit they no longer need. Finding a buyer today means WhatsApp status, estate groups, Instagram, or classifieds.

What breaks

Discovery is random. Prices are guesswork. Payments are “transfer to this account.” Meetups feel unsafe. Deals die in the chat. Fraud is easy.

What ReWorth is for

Turn informal resale into structured, local, trusted commerce — the first place people think of before throwing something away or buying the same item new.

What it is

A Lagos-first recommerce marketplace.

Not a classifieds wall. A mobile-first product (iOS + Android) with a web companion for browse, search, listing, and checkout.

Sell

Photo → AI draft → price range → publish. Ask, offer, or Instant Buy.

Buy

See what is nearby. Chat. Offer. Pay with protection. Pickup or delivery.

Swap / give away

Trade items, or pass them on. Same trust rails as cash deals.

Promise — Sellers: turn unused things into money quickly. Buyers: useful items nearby at better prices. Both: trade with greater confidence.

How it works · seller

Open app. Tap Sell. Live in ~60 seconds.

  1. Photograph the item (camera or gallery).
  2. AI suggests category, title, brand, condition, description, and a price band.
  3. Seller sets price and mode: sell, swap, or give away.
  4. Pick community (Lagos corridors first, then other pilot cities) and fulfilment: meetup, pickup, or delivery.
  5. Publish. Questions, offers, and buy requests arrive in chat.
  6. Accept → buyer pays into regulated escrow-shaped flow → handover → buyer confirms → seller is paid → both rate.

How it works · buyer

Nearby first. Then pay only when it is real.

  1. Home uses city + community so the feed is local, not national noise.
  2. Search, filters, saved alerts, and “for you” rails surface items worth opening.
  3. Product page: photos, condition, seller trust, fulfilment options.
  4. Message or offer. Or buy now / Instant Buy where eligible.
  5. Pay via Paystack-shaped protection (hold → deliver → confirm → release). ReWorth does not sit on customer funds outside a regulated PSP.
  6. Confirm receipt. Review. Dispute path if something goes wrong.

Trust stack

Why this is not “another Jiji clone.”

Trusted people

Phone OTP, optional identity, reviews, trust score, badges. Visible history — not anonymous dump accounts.

Trusted items

Structured listings, photo checks, condition, vehicle inspection, luxury authentication where it matters.

Trusted payments

Escrow-shaped orders on Paystack. Buyer protection fee. Disputes and refunds as a product, not a DM argument.

Trusted collection

Meetup guidance, pickup, platform delivery quotes. Communities and moving sales recreate estate-group density with rules.

Surfaces

Mobile is the product. Web is the companion.

Consumer app

Onboarding, home, search, sell, chat, checkout, orders, communities, moving sales, Ask ReWorth, room scan, referrals, Seller Plus.

Web

Browse, search, listing detail, checkout, account — same journeys for desktop and share links.

Ops

Admin: moderation, verification, reports, KPIs. Partner console for estates. Corporate relocation workspace.

Pilot pickers: Lagos, Ogun, Ibadan, Osun, Ondo, Ekiti, Edo, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano. Marketing stays density-first — Lagos corridors before the whole map.

How we make money

Liquidity first. Then take a thin, fair cut.

Listings, browse, chat, and offers stay free at launch so the market can fill. Monetisation is already in the product (Phase 3.3), gated so we do not tax empty shelves.

Go-to-market

Density, then geography.

Where we start

Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi, VI, Oniru, Chevron, VGC, Ajah. Estates, relocating families, professionals, property managers, corporate movers.

Supply before ads

Seed toward ~500 founding sellers and 2,500–5,000 quality items. Free verification, listings, collection support, featured slots, early-seller badge.

90-day example targets (Lagos geography, recalibrate after beta): 10,000 registered users · 5,000 active listings · 1,000 completed transactions · <1% confirmed fraud · <3 min median time to list.

Sell-well order: Lagos density → live Paystack + Termii → stores + push → founding sellers → then Abuja / PH / Ibadan once those cities have stock. See docs/NIGERIA_MARKET_LAUNCH.md.
