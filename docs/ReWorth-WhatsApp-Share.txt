*ReWorth — Product brief*
_Lagos, your unused things are worth something._

*What it is*
Nigeria’s trusted recommerce marketplace — buy · sell · swap · give away. Mobile-first (iOS + Android), web companion. Photograph an item → list in about a minute → trade nearby with more confidence than WhatsApp groups or classifieds.

*How it works*
*Seller:* Open app → Sell → photos → AI drafts title/category/price band → choose sell/swap/give away → publish → offers in chat → buyer pays (escrow-shaped via Paystack) → handover → confirm → you get paid → both rate.
*Buyer:* Local feed by city/community → search → chat or offer → protected pay → pickup or delivery → confirm receipt.

*How we make money*
Listings/browse/chat stay free early. Revenue after liquidity:
• Buyer protection ~2.5% (cap ₦5,000)
• Boost / Featured listings
• Seller Plus ~₦4,999/mo
• Delivery margin, inspection/auth, consignment
• Later: corporate relocation, estate partners, promoted search

*Roadmap*
*Built:* marketplace core + swap, communities, moving sales, AI assist, Instant Buy, monetization, multi-city pickers (SW, Edo, Abuja, PH, Kano).
*Next:* Lagos supply density → live Paystack + Termii OTP → Play/App Store + push → founding sellers → then market other cities once stocked.

*Risks & mitigation*
Empty market → founding sellers first, don’t ads-blast 10 cities.
Payments/OTP mock → go live Paystack + Termii.
Fraud → ID, escrow, disputes, ops.
Legal → NDPR + PSP (we don’t hold customer funds).
